The questions learners ask first.

How long does a path take?

Most lessons take 15 to 30 minutes. A full path is usually a few hours, and you can stop and pick up where you left off at any time.

Do I have to take the lessons in order?

No. Jump to any lesson from the list on the left. A lesson is only ticked when you click Next lesson on it, so your progress always shows what you have actually finished.

Where is my progress saved?

In this concept, progress is saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your browser data, or switching device, starts you from zero.

Can I take more than one path?

Yes. Many people start with their own role and add a second path later, for example an admin who also runs payables.

Who can earn a certification?

Anyone who finishes a role path can take its hands-on assessment and earn that role's credential.
